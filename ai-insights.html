Customer dissatisfaction signals

Low satisfaction is defined as invoice rating below 3.0. Risk ranking uses observed low-rating share and number of purchases; it is a transparent heuristic, not a trained classifier.

Customers to review

Prioritized using a smoothed low-rating ratio: (low-rated invoices + 1) / (total invoices + 2). Small sample sizes make these scores uncertain.
